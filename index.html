How LearnSnap teaches

Four steps, in this order, every time.

Step 1

Snap

Take a photo or upload up to four images of an assignment, each up to 5 MB. LearnSnap reads it and works out the subject, the topic and how hard it is.

Understand

Everything in the app

AI tutor chat

Ask anything. Try "Explain this in simpler words", "Why is my answer wrong?" or "Quiz me". It explains, then asks you questions back.

Brain games

Short games for memory, focus, speed and logic. They practise thinking skills. They are not an IQ test.

Streaks, XP and levels

Earn XP for learning, build a daily streak, unlock achievements and hit a daily goal you set yourself.

Progress you can read

Charts of your activity, your strongest and weakest subjects, and suggested next steps based on your results.

Your learning library

Search everything you've snapped, practised and asked about, all in one place.

Private by design

Photos are stored privately and each student sees only their own data. Notifications stay gentle.

Questions

When can I download it?

LearnSnap is coming soon to Google Play for Android and the App Store for iPhone. It isn't in either store yet. This page will link to both on launch day.

Will it just give me the answers?

No. LearnSnap teaches the method, offers three levels of hints and checks your attempt. It is built to help you understand.

What can I snap?

Photos of assignments or worksheets as JPG, PNG or WebP, up to four images at a time and 5 MB each. You can also type a question in the tutor chat.

How does it know I've mastered something?

It combines quiz, practice, recent and lesson results, and needs enough attempts first. The scale above shows the levels.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Your progress is saved to your account, so it follows you between devices.

Is it free?

You can start for free, with a daily limit on AI features. A premium plan with higher limits is planned.
