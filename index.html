Questions

Good to know

Will it just give me the answers?

No. LearnSnap is designed to teach. It walks through the method, offers three levels of hints and checks your attempt. Its job is to help you understand.

What can I snap?

Photos of assignments or worksheets as JPG, PNG or WebP, up to four images at a time and 5 MB each. Typed questions work too through the tutor chat.

How does it know when I've mastered something?

It combines quiz, practice, recent and lesson results, and requires enough attempts first. Scores and the levels for Understood and Mastered follow the numbers shown above.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Sign up with your email, or continue with Google in a web browser. Your progress is saved to your account so it follows you between devices.

Is it free?

You can start for free with a daily limit on AI features. A premium plan with higher limits is planned.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 7.0 and newer. An iPhone version is planned. Until it is in the App Store, iPhone users can open LearnSnap in their web browser.
